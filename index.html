Life in Weeks

    Life in Weeks draws your whole life, one dot for every week. Inspired by the essay Your Life in Weeks on Wait But Why and the video When This Number Hits 5200, You Will Be Dead from Kurzgesagt.

    Nothing is stored anywhere. There is no account, no server and nothing kept in your browser: the whole chart is encoded in the page address. Bookmark it or copy the link to keep it, and re-save the bookmark after you change something.

    Enter your date of birth and expected lifespan, then colour the periods that shaped you — school, university, jobs, relationships, the places you have lived — and ring single dates such as a wedding or a move. Share the result as a link or save it as an image.

    Free and open source. Works on phones and desktops. Supports English and Russian. Report an issue.

    Share

    Anyone who opens the link sees the whole chart — every period, date and label — because the link is the data. Nothing passes through a server.
